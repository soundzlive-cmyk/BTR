T.A.R.G.E.T.S. Co-Ed Program

The T.A.R.G.E.T.S. Co-Ed Program is designed to develop discipline, leadership, confidence, and accountability in young people as they navigate the transition into adulthood. Through mentorship, real-life conversations, and structured development, participants are challenged to think differently, take ownership of their actions, and elevate their standards.

Focused on mindset, decision-making, emotional intelligence, communication, and personal responsibility, the program equips both young men and young women with the tools needed to lead themselves, support their families, and positively impact their communities.

This isn’t just about growth…

it’s about becoming the person you’re called to be.

Aim Today. Lead Tomorrow.
