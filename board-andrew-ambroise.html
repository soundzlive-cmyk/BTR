BTR BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Andrew Ambroise

Co-Chair

Andrew Ambroise is a longtime resident of the City of Champions, Brockton, MA. A proud graduate of Brockton High School, Andrew went on to earn a degree in Computer Engineering and Computer Information Systems from Suffolk University. Continuing his education, he later obtained his MBA.

Over the years, Andrew has demonstrated his commitment to youth development by serving as a coach and mentor for the Brockton Community Basketball League. He currently serves as the Senior Director of Software Quality Engineering at Cartera Commerce, a Rakuten company, located in Lexington, MA. Deeply committed to his community, Andrew also serves as the Senior Pastor of Hosanna International Ministries (#IGoToHim) in Brockton.

In addition to his pastoral duties, Andrew provides men’s mentoring and counseling, and he and his wife together offer couples counseling services.
