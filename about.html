About BTR

Mission Statement

At Breaking Through Roadblocks, we empower individuals to overcome obstacles and unlock their full potential.

Grounded in accountability, integrity, communication, and respect, we transform challenges into opportunities.

We are committed to identifying and dismantling the barriers that have traditionally limited growth and success.

GROWTH · CONNECTION · ACCOUNTABILITY

BTR Men’s Monthly Meetings

Breaking Through Roadblocks (BTR) monthly men’s meetings are intentional spaces designed to bring men together for growth, connection, and accountability. These gatherings create an environment where men can have real conversations based on life experiences, improve personal development, and navigate challenges with purpose.

Each meeting is built to:

  • Encourage open dialogue and shared experiences
  • Provide practical tools for personal and professional growth
  • Strengthen community through connection and support
  • Reinforce accountability and consistency in individual journeys

Whether it’s through guided discussions, guest speakers, or group reflection, BTR Monthly Meetings serve as a foundation for building stronger individuals and a more unified community.

AUTHENTICITY · SUPPORT · STRENGTH

BTR Women’s Monthly Meetings

Breaking Through Roadblocks (BTR) Women’s Monthly Meetings are empowering spaces created for women to grow, connect, and lead with purpose. These gatherings are rooted in authenticity, support, and strength, providing a safe environment where women can be heard, challenged, and uplifted.

Each meeting is designed to:

  • Encourage meaningful conversations around personal growth, identity, and life transitions
  • Build confidence, self-worth, and leadership from within
  • Encourage accountability, healing, and resilience
  • Strengthen bonds through shared experiences and sisterhood

Through open dialogue, guided discussions, and intentional reflection, women are equipped with the tools, perspective, and mindset needed to navigate life with clarity, confidence, and purpose.

These are more than meetings. They’re spaces to grow, to heal, to rise, and to step boldly into the woman you are becoming.

BTR Community Events

Breaking Through Roadblocks (BTR) Community Events are designed to bring people together, build connections, and create real impact. These events serve as a bridge to connect individuals, families, and organizations to opportunities, resources, and each other.

TENACITY ALLOWS REACHING GOALS EVEN THROUGH STRUGGLES

T.A.R.G.E.T.S. Mentorship Program

The TARGETS Program is a comprehensive mentorship and leadership development initiative created by Breaking Through Roadblocks (BTR) to equip young people with the tools, mindset, and exposure needed to succeed in life.

At its core, TARGETS is not a one-time program; it is an ongoing ecosystem of development. Through multiple 10-week cycles offered throughout the year, participants are consistently engaged, supported, and challenged at every stage of their journey.

A larger framework of support

These cycles serve as structured touchpoints within a larger framework that includes:

  • Leadership & Life Skills Development — decision-making, emotional intelligence, financial literacy, communication, and accountability
  • College & Career Exposure — college tours, career panels, and workforce readiness through partners like MassHire
  • Cultural & Educational Experiences — Black History tours, educational tours, and experiences that expand perspective
  • Academic Support — SAT/PSAT preparation and guidance toward higher education pathways
  • Community & Networking Opportunities — teen networking events and access to professionals and mentors
  • Ongoing Mentorship & Accountability — continued group discussions and check-ins beyond each cycle
  • Team Building & Leadership Development — hands-on workshops and activities that strengthen collaboration, communication, and trust
  • Group Outings & Experiential Learning — shared experiences that reinforce lessons, build relationships, and create lasting impact
  • Recreation & Engagement — sports and activities that build discipline, teamwork, and leadership

TARGETS is designed to meet young people where they are and grow with them over time. Each cycle builds on the last, creating consistency, reinforcement, and real transformation.

This isn’t just a program—it’s a system of support, a pipeline for development, and a foundation for long-term success.
