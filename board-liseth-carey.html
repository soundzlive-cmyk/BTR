BTR BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Liseth Carey

Special Projects Coordinator

Liseth Carey is a compassionate and dedicated nurse committed to caring for others with empathy, patience, and kindness. She is passionate about guiding and empowering young women, offering wisdom and genuine support wherever she can.

Her faith is the foundation of her life, and she is actively involved in her church community, recently joining the women’s ministry with excitement to grow and serve. Liseth faithfully supports her church and helps wherever she is needed. Beyond healthcare, she is a talented cook and creative event planner with a gift for bringing people together and creating meaningful experiences.

She is also a devoted wife and proud mother of three beautiful daughters. At her core, Liseth leads her home and community with love, faith, and quiet strength.
