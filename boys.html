YOUTH MENTORSHIP

T.A.R.G.E.T.S. Young Men’s Program

The T.A.R.G.E.T.S. Young Men’s Program is designed to develop discipline, leadership, and accountability in young men as they navigate the transition into adulthood. Through mentorship, real-life conversations, and structured development, participants are challenged to think differently, take ownership of their actions, and elevate their standards.

Focused on mindset, decision-making, emotional intelligence, and personal responsibility, the program equips young men with the tools needed to lead themselves, their families, and their communities.

This isn’t just about growth—it’s about becoming the man you’re called to be.

Aim Today. Lead Tomorrow.
