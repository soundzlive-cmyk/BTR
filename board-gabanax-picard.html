BTR BOARD OF DIRECTORS

Gabanax Picard

Events Coordinator Chair

Gabanax Picard is a blessed and highly favored father and son, dedicated to his craft and community. A graduate of Brockton High School, he continued his education at Southeastern Regional and ITT.

With over 20 years of experience in the construction and roadways industry, Gabanax is a proud union worker, a CDL Class A driver, and a certified boom operator. He also utilizes his skills as a handyman, specializing in low-voltage wiring.

Passionate about the outdoors, Gabanax enjoys extreme sports, fishing, rock climbing, and immersing himself in nature.
