Joseph Alves

Youth Coordinator

Joseph Alves was raised in Brockton, MA and graduated from Brockton High School.

After high school, Joseph chose a trade career path in IT (Information Technology) data networking.

Joseph has been climbing the IT career ladder since 2001. Currently, Joseph is a Telecommunications Manager at Massport, where he oversees a variety of phone, network, and mobile systems and devices throughout Logan International Airport.
