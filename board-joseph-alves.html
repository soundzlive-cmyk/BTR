Joseph Alves

Youth Coordinator

Joe Alves was raised in Brockton, MA and graduated from Brockton High School.

Post high school, Joe chose a trade career path in IT (Information Technology) data networking.

Joe has been climbing the IT career ladder since 2001. Currently Joe Alves is a Telecommunications Manager at Massport Authority, where he oversees a variety of phone, network and mobile systems and devices throughout Logan International Airport.
