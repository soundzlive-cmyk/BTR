A Sold-Out Night of Excellence: BTR’s 2nd Annual Gala

On Saturday, November 14, 2026, Breaking Through Roadblocks hosted its 2nd Annual Evening of Excellence Gala at Generations Banquet Hall in Avon, Massachusetts. The night sold out, filling the room with community leaders, families, mentors, partners, and young people, all there to celebrate achievement, resilience, and the people making a difference in our community.

Guests arrived in formal attire paired with their favorite statement sneakers, and the room set the tone: gold chargers, red napkins, black candelabras, and a ballroom glowing in BTR red. Emcee Jonathan Allen kept the energy high from start to finish, guiding the evening through dinner, music, celebration, and the moments that mattered most.

Celebrating our honorees

The heart of the evening was honoring 16 people whose work, growth, and leadership show what it means to break through roadblocks:

  • Young Man of Excellence Award: Joao Rodrigues and Daniel Andrade
  • Young Woman of Excellence Award: Lanai Carey and Jarah Rodrigues
  • Breakthrough Award: Evan Luis Andrade and Terryn Alston
  • Pathway to Purpose Award: Jayden Galvao
  • Rising Star: Kalya Lopes and Darelle Mathieu
  • Pathway Partner Award: Rose Burrows and David Burgo
  • Unsung Hero Award: David Farrell
  • Partnership for Progress Award: Katie Balboni
  • Community Impact Award: Jorge DosSantos
  • The Crown Award: Jessica Castro and Rodney Kornegay

Congratulations to every honoree, and thank you to the families, mentors, and teachers who stand behind them.
