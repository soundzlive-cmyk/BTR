Where Excellence Meets Sole: BTR’s 2nd Annual Evening of Excellence Gala

The ballroom was glowing in BTR red, the sneakers were making statements, and the dance floor stayed busy. On Saturday, November 14, 2026, Breaking Through Roadblocks welcomed community leaders, families, mentors, partners, and young people to Generations Banquet Hall in Avon, Massachusetts, for its sold-out 2nd Annual Evening of Excellence Gala, themed “Where Excellence Meets Sole.”

Gold chargers, red napkins, and black candelabras set the scene, and the guests brought the sneaker ball to life. Formalwear met designer sneakers, classics that helped shape sneaker culture, and one-of-a-kind custom kicks. Zion’s Photobooths & Entertainment stayed busy all night as guests grabbed their people and captured the looks, the laughs, and the moments they wanted to take home.

The opening video, What Is BTR?, drew the room in and laid the foundation for the evening. It showed why Breaking Through Roadblocks exists and what becomes possible when people in our community show up for one another. Then Spirit of Excellence Dance Studio took the stage and brought BTR’s statement poem, Roadblocks to Breakthroughs, to life through dance. Their performance made the struggle, the support, and the breakthrough in those words something the whole room could feel.

Emcee Jonathan Allen kept everyone engaged with his humor and easy presence. He knew when to get the room laughing and when to give an honoree their moment. When it was time to dance, DJ Party P played hit after hit, blending favorites across generations and keeping the dance floor moving. A special thank-you to Evolution Sound and Lighting for the sound and lighting that set the mood all night, from the ballroom glowing in BTR red to the packed dance floor.

Celebrating Our Honorees

At the heart of the evening were 16 honorees. We celebrated young people for their growth and leadership and recognized the mentors, partners, and community leaders who keep showing up and creating opportunities for others.

  • Young Man of Excellence Award: Joao Rodrigues and Daniel Andrade
  • Young Woman of Excellence Award: Lanai Carey and Jarah Rodrigues
  • Breakthrough Award: Evan Luis Andrade and Terryn Alston
  • Pathway to Purpose Award: Jayden Galvao
  • Rising Star: Kalya Lopes and Darelle Mathieu
  • Pathway Partner Award: Rose Burrows and David Burgo
  • Unsung Hero Award: David Farrell
  • Partnership for Progress Award: Katie Balboni
  • Community Impact Award: Jorge DosSantos
  • The Crown Award: Jessica Castro and Rodney Kornegay

The Partners Who Made This Year Possible

We extend a special thank-you to the Senator Charles E. Shannon Community Safety Initiative Grant team and the City of Brockton for standing alongside Breaking Through Roadblocks in our work with Brockton youth. We’re grateful to the Trefler Foundation and Jessica Castro for believing in our mission and providing steady support that helped make this year’s programs and gala possible. We also thank Katie Balboni of Brockton High School for helping bring the Level Up: Career & Confidence Expo to life, giving Brockton youth and young adults the opportunity to connect with local employers, workforce programs, and mentors.
