WELCOME TO BTR

Empowered to overcome
our roadblocks.

At Breaking Through Roadblocks, we believe that every challenge carries the potential for transformation. Our organization is committed to empowering minds, building community, and mentoring growth from setbacks to success.

We provide a private, supportive, and positive space where you can be yourself, identify areas for personal growth, share life experiences, and learn strategies to overcome the obstacles that hold you back.

Through our meetings and community discussions, we foster genuine connections rooted in trust, encouragement, and accountability. Together, we celebrate progress, learn from setbacks, and strengthen the bonds that help each of us rise above life’s challenges.

This is more than a group; it’s a movement committed to becoming better individuals, leaders, and friends.

Join us as we break through barriers, empower one another, and thrive together.

T.A.R.G.E.T.S. · YOUTH MENTORSHIP

Aim today.
Lead tomorrow.

Tenacity Allows Reaching Goals Even Through Struggles.

Every young person deserves someone in their corner. T.A.R.G.E.T.S. gives young men and women a place to be heard, be challenged, and grow, with mentors who show up for them week after week. Through honest conversations, hands-on workshops, and one-on-one guidance, participants build confidence, set real goals, and learn how to bounce back when life gets hard. They walk in unsure of what they can do. They walk out with a plan, a voice, and a community that believes in them.

EVENING OF EXCELLENCE GALA · NOVEMBER 14

Hosted by Jonathan Allen

Jonathan Allen is a dynamic speaker and emcee who has spent more than 25 years owning stages, from conference keynotes to gala ballrooms. Organizers and audiences know him for his warm energy, quick wit, and rare ability to move a room to action. He co-founded Leadership Brainery, a Boston-based nonprofit that has helped more than 3,000 students nationwide on their path to graduate school, and he has raised over $6 million to power that work. A recognized thought leader, he was named Boston University’s inaugural Innovator in Residence and a Boston Business Journal 40 Under 40 honoree. He is a graduate of Grambling State University, SMU’s Perkins School of Theology, and Boston University School of Law. If anyone can attest to the life-changing power of education and leadership, and to our collective capacity to drive impact, it’s Jonathan.
