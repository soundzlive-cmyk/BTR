BTR Breast Cancer Awareness Walk

Moments from the Walk

On October 17, 2026, Breaking Through Roadblocks held its 2nd annual Breast Cancer Awareness Walk at D.W. Field Park in Brockton. More than 80 people came out in pink to walk, show support, and stand together for everyone affected by breast cancer.

Special thanks to Ms. Balboni and the many Brockton High School students who came out to volunteer. Their energy and help made the day possible, and it was inspiring to see so many young people show up for their community.

It was a great event, and we are grateful to every walker, volunteer, and family who joined us. We look forward to seeing you again next year.
