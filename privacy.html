Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Breaking Through Roadblocks, Inc. (“BTR,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Event registration and sign-in: your name, email, phone, the event you are attending, and any optional details you share, such as how you heard about us or which programs interest you.
  • T.A.R.G.E.T.S. intake and consent: participant and parent or guardian contact details, date of birth, age and grade, emergency contacts, medical or allergy notes, media release choice, and signatures.
  • Email: anything you send us at our email addresses.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell your information.

How we use it

  • To plan and run our programs and events and keep participants safe.
  • To contact you about the event or program you signed up for.
  • To send BTR news, only if you ask to receive it.
  • To follow your media release choice when sharing photos or video.

Children’s information

T.A.R.G.E.T.S. serves young people. Information about participants under 18 is collected with a parent or guardian’s consent and is used only to run the program and keep participants safe. A parent or guardian can ask to review, correct, or delete their child’s information at any time.

How information is stored and shared

Form submissions are sent securely to a server operated for BTR and are seen only by BTR staff and volunteers who need them. We do not share your information with outside organizations except when needed to provide a service you requested, when required by law, or to protect someone’s safety in an emergency.

The T.A.R.G.E.T.S. intake form lets you save a draft on your own device. That draft stays in your browser and is not sent to us until you submit the form. You can clear it with the form’s reset button.

Payments and other sites

Ticket and donation payments are handled by PayPal, and our fundraising tracker is hosted separately. Links to Instagram, Facebook, PayPal, and other sites follow those sites’ own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop sending you news, by emailing info@breakingthroughroadblocks.org.

Changes

We may update this policy as our programs change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@breakingthroughroadblocks.org.
