BTR NEWS · 2026-10-10

BTR Game Night

Our Men & Women Game Night brought the community together for an evening built around one big theme: how men and women communicate. The conversations went deep. Guests shared honest perspectives on what we actually hear when the other person speaks, and on the differences in how each of us expresses and receives love. Talking through our love languages opened eyes, sparked plenty of laughter, and gave everyone something real to take home to the people they care about.

Then it was game time. Our men vs. women game covered a wide range of topics, from movies to music, and every round was fun and competitive. Congratulations to our winners from each of our three rounds!

Add great food and an even better vibe, and it was a night to remember. Thank you to everyone who came out, played, and shared.
