Accessibility Statement

Last updated October 12, 2026

Breaking Through Roadblocks, Inc. wants everyone in our community to be able to learn about our programs, attend our events, and get support. That includes people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

What we do

  • We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.
  • Pages include a “Skip to content” link, clear headings, and labeled form fields.
  • Images have text descriptions, and the site can be used with a keyboard alone.
  • Pages adjust to phones, tablets, and larger screens, and text can be zoomed without losing content.

Known limitations

Some older content, such as event flyers posted as images, may not include every detail in text form. If you need information from a flyer, contact us and we’ll share it in another format.

Request help or report a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email us at info@breakingthroughroadblocks.org. Please tell us which page you were on and what happened. We will do our best to respond within 5 business days.

Events

If you need an accommodation to attend a BTR event, let us know when you register or email us ahead of time so we can plan with you.
